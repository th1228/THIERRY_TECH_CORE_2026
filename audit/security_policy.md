## 1. Zero-Retention & Ephemeral Biometrics
All facial landmark mappings and biometric vectors are processed locally within an encrypted memory enclave. No raw images or biometric identifiers are persisted to disk post-session, ensuring absolute compliance with LGPD and GDPR privacy mandates.

## 2. Active Defense Grid (Security Layers)
* **GeoFence & Perimeter Control:** Restricts unauthorized execution nodes outside predefined operational boundaries.
* **Zero-Trust Governance:** Continuous cryptographic validation of system components and identity tokens.
* **Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC):** Advanced protective wrappers designed to mitigate future decryption vectors.
